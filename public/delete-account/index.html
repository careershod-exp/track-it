Delete Your Account

How to request deletion of your data

You can request that your Track It account, and every piece of personal and financial data associated with it, be permanently deleted at any time.

Request deletion by email

Send an email to the address below from the email address linked to your Track It account, with the subject line "Delete my account." We'll confirm your request and complete the deletion.

✉ Email trackituae.com@gmail.com

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email and password)
  • Your profile information (name, country, state/emirate, age range, gender if provided)
  • Every expense, income, savings, and budget entry you've logged
  • Any receipt photos you've uploaded
  • Card payment reminders and notifications

If you're part of a shared ledger

Deleting your account removes you as a member of any shared ledger, but does not delete the ledger itself or entries added by other members — those remain for the rest of that ledger's members. If you're the owner of a ledger and want the entire ledger (and everyone's data in it) deleted, say so explicitly in your request.

How long it takes

We aim to complete deletion requests within 7 days. You'll receive a confirmation email once it's done.
