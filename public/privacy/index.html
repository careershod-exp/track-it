Data & Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

Track It ("we," "our," "the app") is a shared expense, income, and budget tracker. This page explains what personal data we collect, why, how it's stored, and the rights you have over it.

What we collect

  • At sign-up (once): your first name, last name, country, state/emirate, age range, and gender if you choose to share it.
  • Your account: email address and password (stored securely, never in plain text).
  • Financial data you choose to enter: expenses, income, savings, budgets, categories, payment method nicknames, and any receipt photos you attach.
  • Shared ledger data: if you're invited to or invite others to a shared ledger, your display name and activity within that ledger are visible to other members of that same ledger only.

Why we collect it

Your name identifies you to anyone you share a ledger with. Country, state, age range, and gender help us understand our users in aggregate — none of these are ever required to use any specific feature of the app. Your financial entries are used only to run the app for you and anyone you've explicitly shared a ledger with.

Where your data is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, a secure database provider, and is protected by access rules enforced at the database level — meaning you can only ever see data belonging to a ledger you're actually a member of. Receipt photos are stored privately per ledger. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your rights

Under the United Arab Emirates' Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw your consent to its collection, at any time. You can do this directly from the app's Settings, or by contacting us using the details below. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect the lawfulness of anything already processed beforehand.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual expenses, income entries, or an entire shared ledger from within the app at any time. To delete your account and all associated personal data entirely, contact us using the details below.

Children

Track It is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to collect data from anyone under the age of 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change accordingly. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us

For any question about this policy or your data, email us at trackituae.com@gmail.com.
